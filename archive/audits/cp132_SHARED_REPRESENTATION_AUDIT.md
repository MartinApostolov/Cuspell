# Checkpoint 132 — Persistent Shared Representation / Illusion Audit

_Status: advanced spellcraft gap audit 3_

## Trigger

Checkpoint 128 identified persistent multi-observer illusion/representation as a reusable gap. One-shot `Sense` can force or describe a perception event, but Major Image-, Programmed Illusion-, Mirage Arcane-, Project Image-, Mislead-, and similar effects need a shared persistent perceptual carrier.

## Reduction audit

### Sense

`Sense observer channel stimulus` is an observer acquisition EventSpec.

Repeating Sense for every observer does not create one shared object, does not naturally support later observers, and hides observer enumeration.

**Not sufficient.**

### Meaning

`Meaning sign content` states semantic interpretation under language/context.

A painting can mean one thing while visually depicting another; a meaningless pattern can still have a visible representation; an illusion can depict a creature without asserting a linguistic meaning.

Meaning is also intentionally contextual in ordinary language.

**Not sufficient.**

### Form / ShapeLike

Those describe the carrier's actual geometry.

An illusion may visually depict a wall while having no material wall geometry or tactile solidity.

**Not sufficient.**

### World image Kind

The existing image/painting world Kind classifies a useful carrier family, but Kind membership alone does not state arbitrary represented content or channel.

**Not sufficient.**

## Accepted result — Represent

Add:

`Represent(carrier:Entity, content:SemanticValue, channel:SenseChannel) -> StateProposition`

Meaning:

> the carrier presents/depicts the supplied semantic content through the named sensory channel.

It is objective and persistent.

Multiple observers may independently Sense the same carrier under ordinary channel/access physics.

Represent does not imply:

- represented content is true;
- represented Entity exists at the carrier;
- physical Form equals represented Form;
- Matter, collision, mass, Damage, Light, or sound pressure;
- belief or understanding by an observer;
- any unlisted sensory channel.

## Magical shared illusion composition

A minimal visual representation carrier can be created as:

`Create i:Entity Sign $i And Magic $i And At $i Here And Represent $i This Vision!`

Because Create does not imply Matter, this can be a perceptual carrier without silently becoming a solid physical object.

Additional channels are explicit:

`Represent $i This Hearing`

`Represent $i This TouchChannel`

Natural persistence follows from the created Entity and StateProposition. Active maintenance uses:

`Persist Represent This You Vision ThisInterval!`

Programmed behavior can use existing Trigger, Move, Begin/End Represent, or other ordinary EventSpecs.

## Deterministic realization rule

Represent says *what semantic content is presented through what channel*.

The world/media model says how a carrier realizes that presentation.

If the carrier Kind + content + world rules do not determine a sufficiently exact realization, magical execution is incomplete and fails rather than inventing artistic details.

The existing image/painting Kind remains one ordinary visual-carrier family.

## Decision

**ADD Represent as the objective persistent carrier/content/channel relation.**

Do not add spell-name roots such as Illusion, MajorImage, ProjectImage, or Mirage.

Persistent shared illusion is composition over:

- Create;
- Sign/ordinary carrier Kinds;
- Magic;
- spatial placement;
- Represent;
- Persist when active maintenance is needed;
- Trigger/Move/state transitions for behavior.
