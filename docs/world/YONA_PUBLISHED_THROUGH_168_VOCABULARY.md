### Telepathy boundary

Chapter 161 explicitly establishes `«Connection»` as the prerequisite Skill and states that it must evolve twice to obtain `«Telepathy»`.

Therefore **Yona Telepathy is registered as a Skill New Name**.

This does not settle whether generic telepathy is pre-magic Old vocabulary in Cuspell's own history. Core still has no Telepathy Old Name.

## General concepts deliberately not migrated into Yona-only vocabulary

The corpus repeatedly pressures concepts such as Soul, undead/reanimation, Curse, Blessing, Sacred/Divine, healing/regeneration and generic telepathy.

Those are broader world-history/ontology questions. They are therefore not hidden inside Yona's technical package as substitutes for proper core decisions.
