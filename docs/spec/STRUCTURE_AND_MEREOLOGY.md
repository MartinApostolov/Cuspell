# Structure, Parts, Joining, and Wholeness

_Status: current working specification_

## 1. Part

`Part part whole` is the registered structural/mereological relation.

It means the first Entity/value is a constituent part of the second under the applicable structural decomposition.

Part is not numerical Ratio, spatial In, or group Member.

## 2. Transitivity and direct-part meaning

Broad Part may be transitive where the structural domain permits.

A direct/immediate-part meaning is compositional: require `Part A B` and require that there is no distinct intermediate X with both `Part A X` and `Part X B`.

Entity distinctness uses Entity `Equal` under `Not`; the removed Same root is not required.

Exact reflexivity/transitivity rules can be world/ontology specific where structural domains differ.

## 3. Part versus In

Part and spatial containment are independent.

An organ may be both Part of a body and In its occupied Region.

A coin can be In a container without being Part of the container.

## 4. Part versus Member

Group membership uses `Member`.

A member collection can be constructed compositionally:

`Collect :«This»:This Member $«This» They`

This concrete form collects Entities belonging to the `They` collection. It uses a rune-native binder and a Cuspell collection expression, rather than an English `group` placeholder.

A person can be a Member of a social Group without being a structural Part.

## 5. Joined

`Joined A B` is a persistent structural relation indicating an active join/connection under the applicable world structural model.

Specific join types—hinged, fused, bonded, attached, linked, etc.—may be world classifications or narrower vocabulary.

## 6. Begin/End Joined

There is no standalone Join or Separate root.

Joining:

`Begin Joined A B`

Ending the join:

`End Joined A B`

These state-boundary meanings remain objective even when the world determines that realizing them is physically difficult or impossible.

## 7. Composite identity

Beginning Joined does not automatically create a new Entity identity.

Possible world outcomes include:

- two existing Entities remain distinct but Joined;
- the world additionally recognizes a composite Entity whose Parts include them.

Cuspell does not silently choose the second interpretation.

## 8. Whole

`Whole X` is the persistent structural integrity/completeness state relative to X's recognized structural profile.

Whole is not the converse of Part.

`End Whole X` expresses structural loss of wholeness.

A severing-type event can be written as agentive Cause of that state boundary.

## 9. Damage versus Whole

Damage is ordered degree; Whole is a persistent structural state.

They may correlate through world rules but are not synonyms.

An object can be damaged while still Whole, and the exact damage that ends Whole depends on its recognized structure/function.

## 10. Structural world boundary

The world model supplies details such as:

- which Parts are structurally required;
- allowed decompositions;
- join types;
- topology/material constraints;
- composite recognition;
- physical consequences of ending joins/wholeness.

The language provides reusable relations/state transitions; it does not infer an object's engineering model from plausibility.

## 11. Result

The current structural basis is compact:

- Part for mereology;
- Member for group membership;
- In for spatial containment;
- Joined for structural connection;
- Whole for integrity;
- Begin/End for structural state transitions;
- Damage for graded injury.

These distinctions prevent one broad English “part/join/broken” family from hiding several unrelated relations.
