Install CuspellCodexReview-Regular.ttf, open keyboard.html, click glyphs
to compose text, copy and paste into an editor selecting this font.

Idylwild Runic: U+E100–U+E1DF (224 glyphs).
Idylwild Alchemical: U+E200–U+E24A (75 glyphs).

The candidate sprite tiles are imported as solid one-color pixel
outlines and not treated as canonical registered Cuspell runes.
