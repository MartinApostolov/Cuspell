Batch 07 covers emotional states (Happy, Sad, Angry, Fear), attitudes and interpersonal properties, social relationship terms, conduct and selected social/evaluative relations.

**Important small-size risk:** Unlike earlier batches, the remaining source pool cannot supply 49 additional signs without some moderately similar forms. In this proposed 30, the original 234-source geometric audit flags **15 small-size same-orientation comparisons (scores 0.811–0.840)**, all shown in the paired visual sheet and font-preview page. **No new above-threshold pairs** are introduced. These scores do not automatically disqualify the glyphs; they call for manual readability review at intended 32–34 px and 20 px minimum.

If accepted: 198 registered runes plus the special `@` will be assigned (199 designs), leaving **19 registered rune meanings** to assign from **35 unallocated designs**. A tentative 16-shape spare/risk list is recorded in the JSON for planning only; it is NOT approved or locked.

No final semantic font is generated until all 217 registered runes and `@` have approved distinct designs, and the exact 16 spare designs are agreed.
