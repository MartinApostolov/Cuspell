## Deliberate mirror-pair choices

The 35 unused designs contain **18 exact mirrored variants of already-approved glyphs**. Given 19 required meanings and 16 permanent spares, at least two mirrored designs must be used unless earlier approved assignments, the set of 234 designs, or the spare count changes.

This proposal uses **four** mirrored designs to avoid especially confusing variants `A044` (near `A043` and already-approved `A009`) and `R030` (near several approved Runic glyphs). Deliberately related mirror pairings:

- `Part G015` ↔ `Group G016`
- `ThisEvent G021` ↔ `When G022`
- `Rule G121` ↔ `Directive G122`
- `Happen G129` ↔ `Counterfactual G130`

These are oriented mirror counterparts, **not identical drawing positions**, and this association is a *visual mnemonic proposal*, not any claim that the paired meanings are opposites or formally equivalent. Handwritten confusion remains a genuine risk.

The source-similarity audit identifies **11 new above-threshold pairs**, comprising four exact mirrors and seven other close pairs, plus **31 moderate same-orientation small-size pairs**. This is more risk than earlier batches and must not be called "all clear" without human visual approval. The pairs are included in the review material, not hidden.
