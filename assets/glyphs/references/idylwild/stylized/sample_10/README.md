# Cuspell style conversion — Batch 10

**Experimental artwork only; no glyph adoption or semantic assignment.** This batch adapts 13 Runic source glyphs and the **last three Alchemical sources** (A044, A053 and A075). The full Alchemical group of 75 IDs is now stylized; remaining 139 Runic IDs will follow.

[Runic original vs adapted vs existing comparison](runic_comparison.svg) · [Alchemical original vs adapted vs existing comparison](alchemical_comparison.svg) · [Offline viewer](index.html)

Redraws preserve source topology where meaningful, using the established Cuspell 600×1000 em, 66-unit rounded stroke, with sufficient negative space between internal strokes. R057 and R081 received extra spacing in this batch before review because their nested diagonals/arches looked crowded. R074 and R080 are deliberate distinctive motifs but should be checked for near-duplicates alongside prior batches. The simplest R072, R076, and A075 are likely candidate duplicate families to flag during Stage 2 rather than discard prematurely.
