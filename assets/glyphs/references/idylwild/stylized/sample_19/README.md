**All 299 borrowed source IDs now have a candidate vector representation:** 224 Runic, 75 Alchemical. This is *source coverage*, **not** a claim of 299 unique, suitable or finally selected shapes. In particular R113–R224 are alternative source renditions of R001–R112 and share standardized contours.

**Batch 19 visual-styling review is pending.** No candidate has been selected for the canonical inventory, no root-to-glyph meanings have been assigned, and G001–G150 have not been changed. The original CC0 ZIPs, historical glyph drafts, approved spacing fixes and manifests remain preserved.

Once Batch 19 is visually approved, the next distinct work is **Stage 2 inventory audit**: deduplicate second-ink pairs; identify other exact/near-duplicate, mirror and rotation clusters in G/R/A; assess readability at small sizes; then review keep/modify/replace/remove choices in groups. User decisions are required before changing canonical assets. Currently stated minimum for the game is **218 visually distinct signs** (217 roots plus one special), not an approved 256 target.
