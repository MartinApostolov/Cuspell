### R015 lookalikes

The question is justified. The scan links **R015 to R014** (two uprights, same-direction diagonal(s)), plus already-rejected **R087** and **R029** from Stage 2 Group 8. R014 has one diagonal joining uprights; R015 has two parallel diagonals; R087 has one diagonal of a somewhat different slope. R029 differs by opposing diagonal and short spur. R015 also shows coarser similarity to R035 and R052 (Group 22), especially under reflection/rotation, but these have a V-shaped internal branch and different stroke layout.

**No new removals** for R015, R014, R035, or R052 have been inferred. R014 is a live candidate for a future user decision; R087 and R029 remain previously excluded.
