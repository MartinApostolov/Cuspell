# Cuspell glyph stylization — batch 06

This batch contains **16 additional, editable Idylwild candidate SVGs** (8 Runic and 8 Alchemical), presented in the approved Cuspell style. Status: **experimental, not adopted**. All 150 canonical G-series vectors and all original Idylwild ZIPs remain untouched; no meaning or rune-to-root assignment is introduced.

- [Runic original / adapted / current Cuspell comparison](runic_comparison.svg)
- [Alchemical original / adapted / current Cuspell comparison](alchemical_comparison.svg)

Original pixel sprites are embedded directly from the archived Idylwild contact sheet in both SVG comparisons, beside the new editable 600×1000 vector artwork and untouched canonical Cuspell examples at a matched em scale. Each Gxxx in a comparison is a **visual reference**, not a proposed replacement. Source license and checksum documentation: `../../README.md` and `../../original/`.

**Runic:** R006, R011, R018, R025, R033, R037, R051, R069.

**Alchemical:** A025, A029, A032, A038, A039, A040, A041, A042.

### Notes for later distinctiveness audit

- R006 and R018 may be visually close to other stem/twig runes.
- R033 has three relatively compact lobes; confirm small-font legibility in the final inventory.
- R051 is a two-level lattice that might resemble prior framed/symmetric patterns.
- A029's lower rings and A032's horns were widened to prevent the overlapping stroke crowding identified in A050 and A013.
- A038 and A040 contain three tall stems; may be too similar and need differentiation at selection stage.
