# Idylwild borrowed glyph stylization · batch 02

**Non-canonical experimental candidates — not adopted.** The overall 600×1000 em / 66-unit rounded-stroke style was accepted after batch 01, with the A050 lower-spacing correction. This second sample converts 8 further Runic designs and 8 further Alchemical designs.

Open [index.html](index.html), or view [Runic](runic_comparison.svg) / [Alchemical](alchemical_comparison.svg) source-to-vector comparisons. Each row includes actual Idylwild source pixels, a new editable SVG, and an unmodified existing Cuspell glyph. References are visual only; **no source meaning assignments or canonical replacement decisions**.

IDs: Runic R009, R013, R021, R026, R035, R043, R059, R073; Alchemical A002, A006, A009, A015, A018, A023, A033, A057.

The new SVGs are in `svgs/`, source IDs and names in `manifest.json`. Drawings are manual centerline interpretations of the pixel silhouettes, with 66 unit width, round caps/joins, open apertures, and intentional detached marks. In line with the A050 feedback, avoid overcrowded features: R013 / R059 curls have breathing room and A009's diagonal rays do not cross its central stroke intersection.

Original ZIPs in `../../original/` and provenance in `../../README.md` remain unchanged. CC0 declared by Idylwild on the linked OpenGameArt source pages. All G001–G150 vectors and the canonical registry are untouched. This brings the experimental stylized count to 32 of 299, with 267 remaining.

Do not merge/prune/promote solely from these previews. Distinctiveness, mirrors, duplicates, and Keep/Change/Reject decisions come during Stage 2 after stylization.
