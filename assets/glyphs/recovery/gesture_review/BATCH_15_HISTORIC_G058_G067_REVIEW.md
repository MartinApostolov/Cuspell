  (the two glyphs face opposite ways vertically), then horizontal
  crossing bar. They are related orientations rather than a
  simple left-to-right mirror.
- **44 — G062/G063:** **2 strokes each**. Vertical/tilted long stem
  followed by its lower curved branch. They are reflected partners.

  lower outward branch; these retained historic buds were previously
  approved visually. Reflected partner has the reverse x-direction.

  and shorter crossing/descending branch, reflected counterparts.

The original guides contain **filled blue circles for the recorded pen
starts and hollow circles for recorded pen ends**. Path order is
historically archived; these directions are not new proposals.
