**Pending user review — prepared 2026-10-22, not yet approved.**

[Visual guide contact sheet](BATCH_20_HISTORIC_G017_G024_REVIEW.svg)

These eight SVG guides are copied from the earlier preserved v0.13
drawing-guide evidence, not reconstructed lost v0.19 gesture order.

- **63 — G023/G024:** mirrored sweeping angular zigzags. One continuous
  main pen stroke per glyph, **plus a separate solid black dot**, requiring
  an independent pen tap. Start blue near the outer top; finish white
  at the opposite low tip. Original historical guide start/end markers
  document only the long main line, not the dot's placement in time.
- **64 — G021/G022:** mirrored two-stroke signs. First draw the nearly
  vertical downward line, then start separately on the stem and sweep
  out into the folded eye/leaf-shaped line, ending on its lower side.
  The separate second stroke may touch the shaft even though the
  pen is lifted between paths.
- **65 — G019/G020:** mirrored long rounded single-stroke returns,
  looping from the upper outside around a broad top and into a
  lower inward curl; **one continuous stroke** despite complexity.
- **66 — G017/G018:** mirrored long open two-lobed returns, one
  continuous stroke from an outer high tip through the center pinch
  and around the lower curve to the opposite low tip.

**Markers:** filled blue circles are archived starts, hollow white
circles are endpoints; the large black dot on G023/G024 is actual art,
not a start point. That dot's historical drawing order is not specified
by the original path-marker guide.
