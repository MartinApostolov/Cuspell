# Historical handwriting comfort review — four groups (47–50)

**Awaiting user review.** These are preserved v0.13 outlines and their
recovered original pen start/end markers. No new stroke directions or
SVG silhouette changes have been proposed.

[View the full four-group comparison](BATCH_16_HISTORIC_G050_G057_REVIEW.svg).

- **47 / G056–G057:** two paths. Long folded upper-to-lower curved stroke,
  followed by the short, nearly horizontal crossing line.
- **48 / G054–G055:** two paths. Long folded arc, followed by a tall
  central vertical stroke; the curved family resembles G056/G057,
  but differs in its crossing line.
- **49 / G052–G053:** one continuous curved return, making a narrow
  closed leaf-like form without a pen lift.
- **50 / G050–G051:** one continuous broad curved stroke ending in
  an inward curl, without a pen lift.

In the guide panels, filled blue dots are pen-down starts and hollow
circles are the path endpoints. These are preserved historical
direction markers, not new inferred gestures.
