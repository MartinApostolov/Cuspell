# Historical guide comfort review — groups 40–42

**Pending user feedback.** These are original v0.13 SVG glyphs with
historically archived drawing start/end indicators. No source edits.

[View all six glyphs and their original markers](BATCH_14_HISTORIC_G064_G074_REVIEW.svg).

- **40 — G071/G072:** two historical strokes: tall curved return and
  short upper branch. Check the small branch and pen lift.
- **41 — G073/G074:** **one continuous curved path plus a distinct black
  DOT**. The original guide documents the line's direction but does
  not separately specify the dot's painting order. Physically drawing
  the glyph requires a second contact/tap for the dot. The large black
  circle is part of the glyph, *not* a blue start marker. Check its
  placement during fast drawing.
- **42 — G064/G065:** two original strokes: hooked upper sweep then
  separate middle cross-stroke. These two are actual mirror variants,
  despite the unusual adjacent numbering at G063–G065.

Solid blue dots mean historical line starts. Hollow end markers can
be white with dark borders in these older preserved guides. The
large black G073/G074 dot is independent artwork and should not
be confused with a pen-start marker.

**72 older glyphs** remain for optional comfort checks including these
six (about 12 three-group batches). No new guides are missing.
