# Cuspell handwriting / air-gesture review — Batch 1 (three groups)

**All three trajectory groups explicitly approved 2026-10-22 as contemporary guides. Canonical glyph silhouettes and semantic assignments remain unchanged.**

Following completion of all 13 visual comparison groups, the user asked to
start handwriting and gesture testing **three groups at a time**.

This batch examines three deliberately difficult **screenshot-traced**
mirrored pairs: **G111/G112**, **G113/G114**, **G115/G116**.

## How to review

[Open the review contact sheet](BATCH_01_G111_G116.svg). The gray silhouette
is the **unchanged canonical glyph**. Colored lines are *proposed*
pen trajectories and are not exact tracing of the lost original guides.
Numbers mark **pen-down starts**; arrowheads mark proposed stroke ends.
To air-write, trace each numbered stroke continuously, lift, then trace the
next. Mirror partners reflect both the source shape and the gesture directions.

### Group 1 — G111/G112: Outer return + rising inner hook

1. Begin at upper outer tip; sweep down around the bottom and back up on right.
2. Begin at the upper inner tip; curve down toward the bottom join.
   **Target: two strokes, one lift.**
   
Test: Does the large looping motion feel natural, or would another order be easier?

### Group 2 — G113/G114: Folded upper sweep + detached lower diagonal

1. Draw upper folded curve from left to corner, returning down-left.
2. Draw detached lower diagonal from right-middle to lower left.
   **Target: two strokes, one lift.**

Test: Does the sharp fold work in air writing, and are the two parts easy to space?

### Group 3 — G115/G116: Arch/underline + pointed two-leg form

1. Upper arch starts left, curls right and returns as underline **without a lift**.
2. Lower pointed apex -> curved left leg.
3. Return to apex -> curved right leg.
   **Target: three strokes, two lifts.**

This respects the user's earlier upper rounded arch / underline, and
pointed lower shape with two descending branches. The old sketch established
**appearance**, not authenticated stroke timing or order.

## Scope and integrity

- Canonical G111–G116 in `source_geometry.json` remain **unchanged**.
- All guides generated for post-G100 SVGs are currently placeholders;
  **do not replace them with these draft directions without user approval**.
- A screenshot-traced black **filled contour does not encode centerlines**,
  drawing motion or stroke order. The suggested blue/orange/purple lines are
  manually estimated from the visible silhouette; avoid claiming pixel-perfect
  centerline fidelity.
- No SVG font build or dictionary modification is needed for this review.
- Reviewer should assess repeated drawing on paper and in air, mirror comfort,
  stroke count, and whether the proposal still resembles the approved glyph.
